Frequently asked questions

Who will I meet with?

You are matched to the counselor assigned to your program. You will see their name as soon as you select your program in the booking form.

Why do I need to verify my email?

We send a 6-digit code to your school email to confirm it is really you. The code is valid for 5 minutes.

Is my booking confirmed right away?

Your booking starts as Pending. It becomes valid once your counselor confirms it. Keep your receipt or reference code and show it at the Guidance Office.

How do I check my booking?

Enter your reference code in the Check my booking form to see its latest status.
